# Understanding calibration results at the Yellowknife short-period seismometer array

This repository contains inputs, scripts and results relating to a study
of sinewave calibration results.

This material was initially to be presented at the NDC Workshop in Toledo, Spain, 14-17 April 2020, which was cancelled due to the Coronavirus pandemic.

In the end these slides were presented to the Specialized Technical Meeting on Preventive and Predictive Maintenance of the International Monitoring System (IMS), Wednesday, 18 November 2020:
[Ackerley & Gias (2019) slide deck](https://gcdocs.gc.ca/nrcan-rncan/llisapi.dll?func=ll&objaction=overview&objid=56356296)

A detailed white paper is here, perhaps to be turned into a GSC Open File:
[Ackerley & Gias (2019) white paper](https://gcdocs.gc.ca/nrcan-rncan/llisapi.dll?func=ll&objaction=overview&objid=48564055)


## Prerequisites

* pandas
* seaborn
* [catalogue_tools](http://bitbucket.seismo.nrcan.gc.ca/projects/CAT/repos/catalogue-tools/browse/)
